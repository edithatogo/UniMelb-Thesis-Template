\Preface{

	\addtocontents{toc}{\vspace{1em}}  % Add a gap in the Contents, for aesthetics

	The preface must contain:

	\begin{itemize}
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] a detailed description of the contributions the candidate has made to any co-authored publications, draft publications, or publications in submission incorporated into the thesis, including components that the candidate regards as their sole work;
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] where a publication or part of a publication has been used, the place in the thesis where the content has been incorporated (e.g., text on page(s) xx is from [name of publication], or that chapter yy is adapted from [name of publication]). These publications or any draft publications and publications in submission should not be included in the bibliography of the thesis;
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] a description of any work in the thesis that has been submitted for other qualifications;
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] a description of any work that was carried out prior to enrolment in the course;
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] a description of whether any digital editorial assistance or generative AI tools were used in preparing the thesis, and the extent of its use. The declaration is expected to be specific, detailed and exhaustive. Examples of such disclosures include:
		      \begin{itemize}
			      \item Generative AI was used in this thesis to improve clarity and expression. Logs of use including dates and input text are available on request;
			      \item Generative AI was used to create diagrams xx, yy. The prompts and applications used for the generation of these diagrams are noted in the footnote of each image;
			      \item AI tools were used to generate the data visualisation shown in diagram xx. I have confirmed that the visualisations are an accurate reflection of the data. The tool used was yy on date zz.
		      \end{itemize}
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] a description of whether any third-party human assistance was provided in preparing the thesis and whether these parties are knowledgeable in the academic discipline of the thesis. This declaration is expected to be specific, detailed, and exhaustive;
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] the citation and publication status of all publications incorporated into the thesis and appendices using these categories:
		      \begin{itemize}
			      \item Unpublished material in preparation for publication but not yet submitted, including preprints;
			      \item Submitted for publication to [publication name] on [date];
			      \item In revision following peer review by [publication name];
			      \item Accepted for publication by [publication name] on [date];
			      \item Published by [publication name] on [date].
		      \end{itemize}
		\item[\tiny{$\blacksquare$}] an acknowledgement of all sources of funding, including grant identification numbers and acknowledgement of Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarships, including RTP fee offset scholarships, where applicable.
	\end{itemize}

}
